- **Exponential iteration and root bracketing** (Q15): follow the iteration one step at a time and label \(x_1, x_2, x_3\) accurately. Practise the staircase diagram and the change-of-sign argument using endpoint values, continuity, and a rounded root conclusion. You gave \(x_2=1.873\) (this is approximately \(x_3\)); the staircase and interval check were missing.

- **Exponential depreciation models** (Q11): convert years to the model’s time unit before substituting. Here \(t\) is in months, so two years means \(t=24\), not \(t=2\). Then compare the prediction with the observed £320 before judging reliability.
