## Revision priorities

### Definite mistakes (9 marks lost)
1. **Answer exactly what is asked (4 marks: Q3a, Q4a).** If the question says "speed", give a magnitude with Pythagoras. A velocity vector is not a speed. Before boxing an answer, re-read the question stem and check the units and type of quantity.
2. **Displacement versus position vector (2 marks: Q4c).** Position = starting position + displacement. Include A's coordinates (here −10 for j).
3. **Calculus in projectile problems (2 marks: Q5c).** The angle α is constant, so differentiate only the t terms. For maximum height, use v² = u² − 2gs vertically with v = 0, which is faster and avoids calculus.
4. **Modelling questions (1 mark: Q6e).** When a model assumption changes, go back to the original equation (moments about A) and see what changes. Do not reuse the old formula.

### Uncertain marking
- **Q5(c):** the M1 could be 0 under strict marking.
- **Q6(c):** the F = μR B1 relies on being implied.
- **Q3(d):** awarded as benefit of the doubt for the wording.

### Skills to drill
- Vectors (SUVAT in i, j form): position vs displacement, speed vs velocity.
- Projectiles: max height, time to the maximum, and when to avoid differentiating.
- Rod and ladder equilibrium: how moments change when the centre of mass moves, and justifying qualitative changes.
- Exam technique: a final "answer check" line, with the question's key word underlined.
