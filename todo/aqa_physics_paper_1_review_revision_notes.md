## Extra Clarifications on Your Margin Notes

* **Q14 (Photoelectric Effect):** Your reading is spot on. Stopping potential energy $eV_s$ equals the **maximum kinetic energy** ($KE_{\max}$) of a photoelectron, not the incident photon energy:
  $$hf = \phi + KE_{\max} \implies eV_s = hf - \phi$$
  $eV_s$ would only equal $hf$ if the work function $\phi$ were zero.
* **Q18 (Progressive Waves — C is correct):**
  * $\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{330}{660} = 0.5\text{ m}$, and period $T = \frac{1}{660}\text{ s} \approx 1.5\text{ ms}$.
  * **Why B fails:** $25\text{ cm}$ is $\frac{\lambda}{2}$, meaning the points are in **antiphase** ($\pi\text{ rad}$), not in phase.
  * **Why D fails:** A particle reverses direction **twice per cycle** ($2 \times 660 = 1320$ times per second), just as you calculated.
